About this property

Escape to this stunning luxury waterfront estate on the shores of Lake St. Clair in Belle River, ON. This beautifully designed home blends high-end finishes with relaxed lakeside living — from the dramatic wood-slat master bedroom and spa-inspired marble ensuites to the sweeping open-concept living area with floor-to-ceiling views of the water.

Step outside to your private backyard oasis: sink into the cedar hot tub while watching the sun dip below the lake horizon, gather around the stone fire pit with red Adirondack chairs, or dine al fresco under the covered lower patio. The upper pergola deck is perfect for morning coffee with panoramic lake views. Inside, a dedicated office/den with an L-shaped desk and bookshelves makes this ideal for remote workers, while the spacious loft bedroom with a skylight offers a serene retreat. With ample space for up to 8 guests across four bedrooms — including a dramatic master suite with ensuite, a loft bedroom, and waterfront-view bedrooms — this estate delivers a resort-quality experience at an exceptional value.

Professionally cleaned and stocked for your stay. Smart home entry. Superhost care from your hosts Adrian & Barbara.
